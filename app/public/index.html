Is your medicine actually safe?

ask out loud, in your own language

India's regulator publishes its Not-of-Standard-Quality list as English PDFs. A drug restricted in Europe can still sit on the shelf here. And in any Indian hospital, the patient and the doctor often do not share a language. Four things, one pipeline, in Kannada, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or English.

Is this medicine real?

Check a strip against this month's CDSCO spurious and Not-of-Standard-Quality alerts. Answer spoken back in your language, with the regulator's own link.

Second-read a prescription

A doctor dictates the plan. We check every drug for international bans and recalls, and the whole set for interactions and contraindications.

03

Describe symptoms

Say what you feel, in your own words. Get the drug classes approved for it, an urgency, and the questions to ask, never a prescription.

04

Cross the language gap

A migrant patient and a doctor with no shared language. Translate both ways and hand the doctor a structured complaint with red flags.

      Where the answer comes from

      Anakin.io

      Live evidence

      url-scraper on cdsco.gov.in alerts and the WHO falsified-medicine alert index (39,936 characters, 780 alert terms, read fresh).
      search per drug for CDSCO listings and for bans or suspensions by any national regulator.
      Wire tmg_search on Tata 1mg, a typed action that survives their next redeploy.
      openFDA labels, interactions, boxed warnings and recalls, fetched through the same scraper.

      The language

      saaras:v3 with mode=codemix, because "Dolo 650 safe hai kya" is how people actually speak.
      sarvam-105b reads the evidence and is never asked to recall a drug fact.
      sarvam-translate:v1 across eleven Indian languages, indic to indic.
      bulbul:v3 speaks the answer, because the person who needs this may not read at all.

      Honesty

      What it will not do

      If a source is unreachable the answer degrades to unchecked, not a clean bill, and that answer is never cached. An unavailable answer is never presented as a safe one.

      A missing database entry is ranked as a weak signal and can never become the headline over a real interaction or a regulator ban.
